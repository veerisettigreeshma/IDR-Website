About IDR

An Industry-Led Institute for the Risk Age

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute specialising in digital, cyber and technology risk roles across regulated and high-consequence environments including financial services, critical infrastructure and public sector organisations.

Unlike traditional training providers, IDR bridges the gap between academic research and operational practice. Through a formal UK university partnership, our curriculum is grounded in cutting-edge research while every programme is shaped by practitioners who work on the frontline of digital risk every day.

Our graduates don't just learn — they are assessed on real projects, mentored by industry leaders, and deployed into roles where they can make an immediate impact.

Our Model

Three Pillars. One Mission.

IDR operates across three interconnected pillars — training, innovation and advisory — forming a complete ecosystem for digital risk capability.

Academy

Intensive bootcamps, structured pathways and continuing professional development for students and working professionals entering or advancing in digital and cyber risk roles.

Training · Bootcamps · CPD

Innovation & Incubation

Developing proprietary IP, next-generation risk models and AI governance frameworks. IDR's incubation lab partners with organisations to prototype future-ready risk tools.

IP Development · AI Governance · R&D

Advisory Services

Helping regulated organisations implement and mature risk frameworks including NIST CSF, ISO 27001, NIS2 and DORA — with pragmatic, deployment-ready guidance.

NIST · ISO 27001 · NIS2 · DORA
